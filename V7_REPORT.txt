INTERPRETATION / RECOMMENDATION
Tikhonov-Scaled is a promising numerical core for mixed noise: same solution, independently certified.
Stronger LSQR preconditioning improves the relative-only reconstruction in this control, but remains uncertified.
POCS-CDMA-Dual certifies the mixed case at the same minimum-norm solution: optimizer improvement, no accuracy improvement.
The broad POCS-CDMA solution reflects its bin-mass norm prior and simultaneous point bands; it misses the shape targets.
TV-PD needs more than the short budget for a strict feasible-dual gap. Independent parameter selection still needs verification.
No new arm in the primary controls meets all numerical AND accuracy targets. No universal best solver is established.

LIMITS
Finite caps: failure is not proof of infeasibility and not scientific success.
Two representative stored controls with one noise realization; no robustness claim for all 27 cases.
No relaxed noise weights, physical bounds, or stopping tolerance to force a passing result.
TV-Morozov is a different discrepancy formulation; no alpha sweep is claimed.
Numerical convergence does not guarantee an accurate reconstruction in an underdetermined inverse problem.
Lognormal-fit is excluded from evaluation and remains registered in the full configuration.
Morozov budget validity is audited independently of optimisation; exact declared ball is used for its data check.
Original metadata before that correction is archived in outputs/solver_controls/metadata_before_audit.
The optional POCS fmincon fallback was disabled in the real controls; no performance claim for that branch.
